Setuno

Understand any webpage without leaving it.

Setuno is a Chrome extension for people who read long articles, documentation, papers and tutorials. Highlight something you don't follow and Setuno explains it right there on the page, with no copying, pasting or switching tabs.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

What it does

Define a word

Its meaning in the sentence you found it in, said plainly, with an example.

Explain a passage

What a highlighted passage actually means. Then simpler, or with an example.

Summarize the page

The article's main points in a few sentences, and the reading time that saved.

Setuno uses AI to write its answers. Nothing happens until you ask: it never runs on a page by itself.
